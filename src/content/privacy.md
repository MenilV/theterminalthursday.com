# Privacy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

The Terminal Thursday is a free weekly newsletter run by Menil Vukovic. This page covers what the site and the newsletter collect, who processes it, and how to have it removed. Questions go to [hello@theterminalthursday.com](mailto:hello@theterminalthursday.com).

## The newsletter

- **What we collect:** the email address you enter in a signup form or in the terminal's `subscribe` command, and which of those you used.
- **Who stores it:** [Loops](https://loops.so/privacy), the US-based service that sends the newsletter.
- **What it's for:** sending The Terminal Thursday. Your address isn't sold or shared, and it's used for nothing else.
- **Opens and clicks:** Loops records when an issue is opened and which links are clicked. That shows which tools readers care about.
- **Unsubscribing:** every issue has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. You can also email us and we'll remove you.

## Site analytics: Microsoft Clarity

The site uses Microsoft Clarity to see how people use it, mostly to find layout bugs on phones and older browsers and to spot where signing up is awkward.

- **What it records:** clicks, scrolling, mouse movement and the pages you visit, plus your browser, device and approximate location (from your IP address). Clarity turns this into heatmaps and replays of visits.
- **What it masks:** anything you type into a form field, including your email address.
- **Labels the site adds:** which theme (Windows or Unix) and reading view you used, whether a signup went through, and whether a page crashed. Your email address is never sent to Clarity.
- **Who processes it:** Microsoft, under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). Recordings are kept for 30 days. Heatmaps, and a random sample of recordings, are kept for up to 9 months.
- **Cookies:** if you visit from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, Clarity sets no cookies, because the site never asks for cookie consent. Elsewhere it sets `_clck` (kept up to a year) and `_clsk` (links the pages of one visit) on this site, and Microsoft may set its own cookies on clarity.ms.
- **Opting out:** a content blocker, or your browser's tracking protection, stops Clarity. The site works the same without it.

## Hosting

The site runs on Cloudflare, which sees your IP address in order to serve pages and block attacks. Images in issues load from Loops' image server. The font is served from this site, not from Google.

## Your rights

The newsletter relies on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing. Analytics rely on our interest in keeping the site working.

You can ask what we hold about you, or have it corrected or deleted, by emailing [hello@theterminalthursday.com](mailto:hello@theterminalthursday.com). Clarity data isn't linked to your name or email address, and Microsoft only lets a site delete all of it at once, so it expires on the schedule above. If you're in the EU or the UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.
