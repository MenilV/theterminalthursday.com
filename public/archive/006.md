# 🗞️ Issue #006: The Decision Engines (System One AI)

Everyone is obsessed with chatbots that can write a Shakespearean sonnet, but real production software doesn't need poetry. It needs reliable, lightning-fast decisions.

With the launch of TypeSafe AI's Jev model, the industry is officially shifting toward "System One" AI: models designed to take unstructured data and output typed, probabilistic decisions instead of conversational strings.

Because Jev is proprietary, an absolutely massive open-source ecosystem has exploded around it over the last week. Developers are building autonomous web agents, trading bots, and developer tools that leverage this instant decision-making capability.

Jev is fast 20-200x faster, 40-400x cheaper (with output tokens free) and is optimized for decisions. A perfect development tool.

Here is how the open-source community is reacting to the System One movement.

## ☕ The Curious Non-Dev: No Code Required

What exactly is "System 1 vs System 2" AI?

If you are confused by all this jargon, think of Daniel Kahneman’s famous psychology book, *Thinking, Fast and Slow*.

**System 2** is your slow, methodical brain. If someone asks you to solve 17 x 24, you have to stop and work it out. This is exactly what ChatGPT and Claude are doing—slowly generating reasoning, one word at a time.

**System 1** is your fast, instinctive brain. If someone throws a baseball at your head, you don't calculate the wind resistance; you just duck. Jev is a "System 1" AI. It doesn't write essays; it just looks at data and instantly makes a hard decision. And for most software, that is infinitely more useful.
