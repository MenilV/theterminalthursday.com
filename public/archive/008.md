## 🔬 The Sandbox: 3 Projects I Tested This Week 



### 1. Maybe 💰 (Finance OS) 





![Image](https://images.vialoops.com/cmpfndk6x026w0jvslad39ftq/cmuz98ey40dqn0j25q0fma3ge.png)

What is it? A wildly popular, beautifully designed open-source operating system for wealth management and personal/business finance. It gives you full ownership over your financial data. 



Why you should care: Financial aggregators make their money by selling your transactional data to third parties. "Maybe" allows you to self-host a completely private, localized dashboard to track your net worth, run financial projections, and categorize spending without handing your banking data to a marketing firm. 



My Rating: Absolute Game Changer 5/5 ⭐ Website: github.com/maybe-finance/maybe 💻 Get Started: 

docker run -d -p 3000:3000 -e SECRET_KEY_BASE=YOUR_SECRET_KEY maybeapp/maybe:latest 

Note: It's no longer actively developed! 



### 2. Metabase 📊 (Business Intelligence) 





![Image](https://images.vialoops.com/cmpfndk6x026w0jvslad39ftq/cmuz98rx70d4r0j4em9yyn5e2.png)

What is it? An incredibly powerful open-source business intelligence server that plugs directly into any database (PostgreSQL, MySQL, Snowflake) and lets anyone visualize data instantly without writing a single line of SQL. 



Why you should care: Companies large sums on enterprise BI tools, locking their analysts into proprietary ecosystems. Metabase allows economists and data analysts to spin up complex interactive dashboards and internal reporting tools entirely on open-source infrastructure. And personalise it for their own needs. 



My Rating: Essential Infrastructure 5/5 ⭐ Website: github.com/metabase/metabase 💻 Get Started: 

docker run -d -p 3000:3000 --name metabase metabase/metabase 



### 3. ERPNext 🏭 (Enterprise Resource Planning) 





![Image](https://images.vialoops.com/cmpfndk6x026w0jvslad39ftq/cmuz98lhq04g10j1y8mfe3rjo.png)

What is it? A monolithic, open-source Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system built by Frappe. It comes loaded with modules for accounting, HR, manufacturing, CRM, and inventory management. 



Why you should care: ERP software is the backbone of the physical economy, but the market is heavily monopolized by legacy giants like SAP and Oracle who charge exorbitant licensing fees. ERPNext proves that a modern, open-source framework can actually run the financial and logistical operations of a massive enterprise securely and for free. 



My Rating: Flawless Execution 5/5 ⭐ Website: github.com/frappe/erpnext 💻 Get Started: 

docker run -d -p 8080:8080 frappe/erpnext 







## ☕ The Curious Non-Dev: No Code Required 



What is Open-Source Enterprise Software? When a business buys a software subscription (like Salesforce or SAP), they are paying to "rent" the solution on someone else's server. They don't own the software, and they don't truly own the data format. 



"Open-Source Enterprise" defies this. The source code for massive tools is published for free on the internet. A company can download the code, install it on their own private servers (self-hosting), and use it forever without paying a subscription. This gives businesses ultimate data privacy, total customization, and zero risk of the software company suddenly doubling their prices.
