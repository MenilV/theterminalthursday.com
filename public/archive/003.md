## ☕ The Curious Non-Dev: No Code Required

### How to defend against Voice Cloning scams
With tools like OpenVoice requiring just 3 seconds of audio to perfectly mimic a loved one, "grandparent scams" (where scammers call acting like a family member in distress) are skyrocketing. You don't need code to protect yourself, you need a protocol and a bit of scepticism.

Establish a "Safe Word" with your family. If someone calls from an unknown number, even if they sound exactly like your spouse, child, or parent—ask for the safe word. AI can clone a voice perfectly, but it cannot guess a pre-established offline secret.

## 📡 The Radar: 12 More Projects to Spin Up This Weekend

### 🗣️ The Voice Cloners
1. **[Bark](https://github.com/suno-ai/bark)** *(31,200+ Stars)* - A transformer-based text-to-audio model created by Suno. It can generate highly realistic, multilingual speech as well as background noise, simple sound effects, and non-verbal communications like laughing and sighing.
2. **[RVC-WebUI](https://github.com/RVC-Project/Retrieval-based-Voice-Conversion-WebUI)** - The wildly popular framework for real-time voice conversion.
3. **[CosyVoice](https://github.com/FunAudioLLM/CosyVoice)** - Multi-lingual voice cloning built by Alibaba's FunAudioLLM team.
4. **[TTS](https://github.com/coqui-ai/TTS)** - A massive, foundational deep learning toolkit for Text-to-Speech.

### 🎭 The Visual Illusionists
1. **[DeepFaceLab](https://github.com/iperov/DeepFaceLab)** *(45,100+ Stars)* - The absolute gold standard of deepfake creation. This is the heavy-duty software used by 95% of deepfake creators online.
2. **[Roop](https://github.com/s0md3v/roop)** *(25,300+ Stars)* - One-click face replacement in video (the spiritual predecessor to FaceFusion).
3. **[sd-webui-reactor](https://github.com/Gourieff/sd-webui-reactor)** - The definitive fast and simple face swap extension for Stable Diffusion.
4. **[Rope](https://github.com/hillobar/Rope)** - A GUI-focused deepfake app built on InsightFace that allows for multiple face swapping in a single video.

### 🧍 The Digital Avatars
1. **[SadTalker](https://github.com/OpenTalker/SadTalker)** *(10,500+ Stars)* - Learning realistic 3D motion coefficients for stylized audio-driven single-image talking face generation.
2. **[EMO](https://github.com/HumanAIGC/EMO)** - Emote Portrait Alive: generating expressive portrait videos strictly from audio.
3. **[V-Express](https://github.com/TencentARC/V-Express)** - Tencent's open-source framework for conditional dropout in audio-driven talking face generation.
4. **[MuseTalk](https://github.com/Alibaba-MIIL/MuseTalk)** - Real-time, high-quality audio-driven lip-syncing for digital avatars.
